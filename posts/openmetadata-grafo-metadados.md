  body: |
    **OpenMetadata** is an open-source platform that connects to all your warehouses, databases, BI tools, and pipelines, building a unified metadata graph of every table, column, dashboard, job, and model in your company.

    With that graph, you can follow end-to-end lineage in seconds to see what breaks before you change a field, attach data quality tests where they matter, route incidents to the right owners, and give AI copilots safe, well-described tables to query instead of a swamp of unnamed datasets.

    Integrates with Airflow, Kafka, Spark, Snowflake, BigQuery, Looker, Power BI, and more.
