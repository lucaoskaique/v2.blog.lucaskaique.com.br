    It parses 250+ OSM keys into full 3D scenes with PBR materials and multi-LOD output, exporting glTF, OBJ, or 3D Tiles. That last format is the strategic one: it pipes volunteer-mapped geometry straight into CesiumJS or any OGC viewer. No proprietary pipeline.
